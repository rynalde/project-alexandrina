### Branch A — generative world models (predict pixels)

**World Models (Ha &amp; Schmidhuber, 2018).** Three parts: V, a VAE that compresses each frame; M, a recurrent network that predicts the next compressed frame; C, a tiny controller. In VizDoom the controller was trained entirely inside the model's "dream", then transferred back to the real game. It popularised the term "world model".

**Dreamer (Hafner et al., 2020 → DreamerV3, 2023).** Learns latent dynamics with a pixel-reconstruction loss, then learns to act by imagining futures in latent space. DreamerV3 collected diamonds in Minecraft from scratch.

**MAE (He et al., 2021).** Not a video world model, but the masking blueprint: hide 75% of image patches and reconstruct their pixels.

### Branch B — joint-embedding (predict representations)

**BYOL (Grill et al., 2020).** Predict one view's representation from another view's. EMA target, predictor, no negatives — the anti-collapse recipe from chapter 03.

**data2vec (Baevski et al., 2022).** A student sees a masked input and predicts the representations an EMA teacher produces from the full input — for speech, images and text. The I-JEPA paper names it, together with Context Autoencoders, as its closest prior work.

**JEPA (LeCun, 2022).** A position paper, *A Path Towards Autonomous Machine Intelligence*: predict in representation space, stack JEPAs into a hierarchy (H-JEPA), and put that world model at the centre of an agent.

**I-JEPA (2023) → V-JEPA (2024) → V-JEPA 2 (2025).** Meta's implementations: images, then video, then video at scale with robot planning. Chapters 05–07 open each one up.

### What's new, 2024–2026

The representation branch kept growing fast. Each line below was checked against the paper's abstract:

- — **DINO-WM (Nov 2024).** A world model on top of frozen DINOv2 features, trained on offline trajectories: it plans toward a goal image by searching in feature space — no reward model, no expert demonstrations, no pixel reconstruction.
- — **Intuitive physics (Feb 2025).** Show a possible and an impossible clip; "surprise" = prediction error. V-JEPA shows object permanence and shape consistency where pixel predictors and multimodal LLMs stay closer to chance. A reality check followed: on IntPhys 2 (Jun 2025), in complex scenes most models are still near chance while humans are near-perfect.
- — **LeJEPA (Nov 2025).** SIGReg replaces EMA and stop-gradient (chapter 03): one hyperparameter, about 50 lines of code, 79% on ImageNet with a ViT-H/14.
- — **VL-JEPA (Dec 2025).** Predicts the *embedding* of an answer instead of generating its words: 50% fewer trainable parameters than a comparable token-predicting model.
- — **V-JEPA 2.1 (Mar 2026).** Dense features — the loss uses visible and masked tokens — and 20 points better real-robot grasping than V-JEPA 2-AC.
- — **LeWorldModel (Mar 2026).** An end-to-end JEPA world model from raw pixels with just two losses (next-embedding prediction + SIGReg): about 15M parameters, one GPU, a few hours.
- — **LeVJEPA (Aug 2026).** SIGReg for video pretraining: at ViT-S/B/L scale, on the same data and epochs, it matches or beats V-JEPA 2 with 5.6–20.8× less pretraining compute.

The pixel branch did not stand still either: NVIDIA's **Cosmos** (Jan 2025) released open-weight video-generating world models — the planning baseline V-JEPA 2-AC beat on speed in chapter 07 — and DreamerV3 appeared in *Nature* in 2025.

### What JEPA inherited

- — From <Highlight>World Models / Dreamer</Highlight>: the goal — a model of the world an agent can plan with.
- — From <Highlight>MAE</Highlight>: masking as the pretext task, at scale, with ViTs.
- — From <Highlight>BYOL</Highlight>: the EMA target + predictor that prevents collapse.
- — From <Highlight>LeCun 2022</Highlight>: the insistence on predicting in representation space.

  Generative world models (World Models, Dreamer, Cosmos) and masked autoencoders (MAE) learn by reconstructing pixels; the joint-embedding line (BYOL → JEPA → I-JEPA → V-JEPA → V-JEPA 2 → V-JEPA 2.1) learns by predicting representations — and since 2025, LeJEPA's SIGReg offers a way to do it without EMA or stop-gradient.

<Marginalia label="traps">
  Dreamer's policy imagines in latent space, but its world model is still trained with pixel reconstruction — don't file it under JEPA. MAE masks like I-JEPA but predicts pixels. The 2022 JEPA paper is a proposal, not a trained model. LeJEPA's SIGReg is an anti-collapse regularizer, not a new architecture.
</Marginalia>
